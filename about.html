About Built by finance people, for finance teams

We're rebuilding the finance stack from the ledger up.

Stackly started in 2019 with one stubborn belief: the numbers a business runs on should never be a week out of date. Today a small, obsessive team in Salem and 42 countries builds the workspace where more than 12,000 finance teams close, forecast and decide.

Our story

It began with a close that took nine days

Our two founders were a controller and a platform engineer who kept meeting at the same place: a spreadsheet that was always one import behind reality. Reconciling three bank accounts took a week, the forecast was guesswork by Thursday, and nobody trusted the number the board saw.

So they built the tool they wanted — a ledger that ingests transactions as they land, matches them automatically and reports in real time. Stackly has grown into a team of finance operators, accountants and engineers who ship one product obsessively, with every feature traced back to a real close, a real report and a real decision.

  • Founded by a controller and an engineer who closed the books themselves
  • Profitable-minded: every roadmap item must earn its place in the ledger
  • Remote-first with our head office in Salem and hubs in London and Singapore

What we believe

Four values that show up in the product

They shape how we write code, how we run the books and how we answer a support ticket at 5pm on close day.

Radical clarity

Every number traces back to a source. No hidden fees, no black-box scores, no report you need a translator to read.

Own the number

We don't hand work downstream and hope. Whoever ships a metric stays with it until it reconciles.

Automation over admin

If a human is doing it twice, we build the machine. Matching, reminders and controls should run themselves.

Earn trust daily

Backups, audits, permissions and uptime are renewed every day — trust is a practice, not a certification.

The journey

From one spreadsheet to 12,400 teams

Seven years of shipping the boring, important things — one milestone at a time.

2019

Stackly is founded in Salem, Tamil Nadu

A controller and a platform engineer start building a ledger that reconciles itself. The first version runs the close for three friendly companies.

2021

Our first 1,000 teams

Automatic bank feeds, OCR receipt capture and one-click statements go live. Stackly closes its first full year of revenue and opens a London hub.

Life at Stackly

Small team, high craft, zero finance theatre

We're a team of about 90 people who like hard problems and honest dashboards. Engineers sit with controllers, designers shadow month-end closes, and every all-hands starts with the same slide: how long did our customers take to close this month?

  • Everyone ships — founders review pull requests and support tickets too
  • One all-company close day each quarter so we feel our own product
  • Write it down: decisions live in RFCs, not in someone's memory
  • Protect the deep-work hours — meetings are the exception, not the default
