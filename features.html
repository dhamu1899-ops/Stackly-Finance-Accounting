FAQ

Questions finance teams ask us first

Short answers to the things that decide a switch. Anything else, our team will answer on a 20-minute call.

How long does it take to switch to Stackly?
Most teams are live in under a week. Guided migration imports your chart of accounts, customers, suppliers and open invoices from QuickBooks, Xero or CSV, and a real onboarding specialist configures tax rules, categories and approval workflows with you.
Can Stackly replace QuickBooks or Xero entirely?
For most growing teams, yes — Stackly covers the general ledger, invoicing, expenses, bank reconciliation, tax reporting and the close itself. If you need to keep an incumbent tool for a subsidiary, we sync both ways so nothing is duplicated or lost.
Does it handle multi-currency and sales tax?
Bill and get paid in 135+ currencies with daily FX rates, automatic realised gain/loss handling and consolidated reporting across entities. Tax rates, VAT/GST treatment and registration thresholds are configured per region and applied at line level.
How do the automations decide what to post?
Every rule is explicit and editable. Stackly learns from how you have coded a merchant before, proposes an account and tax rate with a confidence score, and only posts automatically once you approve the rule. Everything else is routed for review with its receipt, source and audit trail attached.
How is my financial data protected?
TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest and encrypted nightly backups, hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centres. SSO/SCIM, enforced 2FA and role-based permissions keep access tight, and read-only seats let your accountant or auditor work safely inside the same workspace.
