Billing FAQ

Questions before you subscribe

Everything about trials, billing cycles, migration and refunds. Anything else, our team answers in under four hours.

How does the 14-day free trial work?
Every plan starts with a full-featured 14-day trial — no credit card required. You get unlimited bank connections from day one, so you can import real history, run a real close and invite your team before you choose a tier. At the end of the trial you can subscribe or stay on a free read-only account; your data is never deleted.
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Yes, in either direction and at any time. Moving to annual billing applies the 20% discount immediately and we prorate the difference on your next invoice. Switching back to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual term — you keep the discount you already paid for.
Can you migrate us from QuickBooks, Xero or spreadsheets?
We import chart of accounts, customers, vendors, open invoices, bills and historical journals from QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite and CSV/Excel exports. Growth and Scale customers get a guided migration session with an onboarding specialist; Scale includes a dedicated CCM who runs the cutover plan with you. Most teams are live within a day.
What is your refund policy?
Every subscription is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. If Stackly is not right for your books, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we refund it in full — no forms, no exit interview. After 30 days you can still cancel at any time and we stop billing at the end of the paid period.
Do I have to pay for every user?
No. Starter includes 3 seats, Growth includes 10 and Scale is unlimited. Extra seats are $9 per user per month, and accountant, auditor and adviser seats are always free and read-only — invite them at no cost so nobody has to email you a copy of the ledger again.
Are taxes included in the listed price?
Listed prices are in USD and exclude VAT, GST and sales tax, which are added at checkout based on your billing country. We issue a compliant tax invoice for every payment, and VAT/GST IDs can be added to your billing profile so the charge lands in your purchase-ledger the way your accountant expects.
