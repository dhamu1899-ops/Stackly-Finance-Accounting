Questions we hear every day

If your answer isn't here, send the form above — a specialist replies within one business day.

How fast will you reply to my request?
Every demo request gets a reply from a finance specialist within one business day. Existing customers reach a human on support in under four hours during business hours, and our emergency line is staffed 24/7 for anything that blocks your close.
Can you help us migrate from QuickBooks, Xero or spreadsheets?
Yes — every plan includes a guided migration call. We map your chart of accounts, import historical transactions and reconcile the first period together, so your opening balances in Stackly tie back to the system you're leaving. Most teams are fully moved in a single day.
Do you complete security questionnaires and vendor reviews?
We do. Send your questionnaire to hello@stackly.com and we'll return it with our SOC 2 Type II report, penetration test summary and DPA within two business days. Stackly is GDPR ready, PCI DSS compliant and ISO 27001 certified, with SSO/SCIM and granular role permissions available on every plan.
I'm already a customer — where do I get support?
Open the in-app help centre to reach your support queue directly, or call +91 422 455 0142 during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9:30am–6:30pm IST). Your account also includes a named onboarding contact for the first 90 days, so you always know exactly who to ask.
